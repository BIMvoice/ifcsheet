Legends on the Wall. The project drawer has a Legends group after Schedules: click a legend to go to its sheet, double-click to rename it, the cross deletes it (and takes it off every sheet), and "+ Legend" opens a short form. The form offers materials, lines and layers, hatches, symbols and annotation types as large toggles, starting with materials and hatches, and covers the whole project or, when a sheet is open, that sheet. A legend made while a sheet is open is put on that sheet at once, at paper size, in the free place nearest the bottom-right so it does not cover a drawing when there is room. The viewport card's "Place on this sheet" list offers legends next to drawings and schedules, and the card of a placed legend changes its title, columns and categories for every sheet it is on. The sheet prints and exports it in the PDF and DXF with the rest.
